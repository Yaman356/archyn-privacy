ECHOES – Support

Archyn Tech · com.archyntech.echoes · iOS & Android

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Write to info@archyntech.com. Please include your device model, OS version and the game version (Settings screen, bottom line). We usually reply within a few days.

FAQ

How do I play? Hold and drag; the orb follows your finger. Reach the crystal before time runs out. Your path becomes an echo — don't touch it. The "?" button on the main menu shows all rules.

The leaderboard only shows my device records. Sign in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) from the Leaderboard screen. The world ranking appears after your first submitted score.

Can I turn off music, sounds or vibration? Yes: main menu → ⚙ Settings. You can also change the language there.

Ads. Ads never interrupt a round. Optional rewarded ads give a second chance or bonus crystals; a short ad may appear between games.

My progress. Progress is stored on your device only; uninstalling the app deletes it.
