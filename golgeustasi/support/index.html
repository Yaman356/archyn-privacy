Gölge Ustası – Support

Archyn Tech · com.archyntech.golgeustasi · iOS & Android

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Write to info@archyntech.com. Please include your device model, OS version and the game version (Settings screen). We usually reply within a few days.

FAQ

How do I play? Drag to rotate the object in front of the lamp until its shadow matches the silhouette on the wall. Tap a hint if you get stuck. A short tutorial runs on first launch.

Can I turn off sound or vibration, or change the language? Yes: Settings (⚙). The game is available in Turkish, English, German, Chinese and Arabic.

Ads. Ads never interrupt gameplay. Optional rewarded ads give bonuses; a short ad may appear between rounds.

My progress. Progress is stored on your device only; uninstalling the app deletes it.
